# Higher Folio

Make your business look as good as it is.

A local-first, phone-friendly invoice maker for the LOQ. Open `index.html` in Chrome or Edge. Bills and business details stay in that browser on this laptop; use **Save as PDF** to create a shareable bill through the system print dialog. On a phone, the fixed bottom button keeps PDF saving within reach, and the browser's Print/Share panel can save or share the PDF.

Add an Instagram handle during the first setup to place it alongside the business contact on every bill. **Copy a friendly message** prepares a short note to paste into an Instagram or WhatsApp conversation after sharing the PDF.

The included dirham graphic is retained in `assets/` for future placement in the invoice template.
